AI-2 PROMPT ENGINEERING — EXPERIMENT 1: ZERO-SHOT VS FEW-SHOT

Task: Classify feedback as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
Input: "The application is easy to use, but it takes too long to load."

ZERO-SHOT PROMPT:
Classify the following customer feedback into one of these categories: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

Feedback: "The application is easy to use, but it takes too long to load."

Return only the category and a one-line explanation.

ACTUAL OUTPUT:
Category: Neutral
Explanation: The feedback balances a positive remark about ease of use with a negative complaint about slow loading times.

FEW-SHOT PROMPT:
Classify customer feedback into Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

Examples:
Feedback: "The delivery was fast and the product is excellent."
Output: Positive
Feedback: "The product stopped working after two days."
Output: Negative
Feedback: "The package arrived yesterday."
Output: Neutral
Feedback: "Customer support was helpful and resolved my issue."
Output: Positive

Now classify:
Feedback: "The application is easy to use, but it takes too long to load."

Return only:
Category: [Positive/Negative/Neutral]
Reason: [One short sentence]

ACTUAL OUTPUT:
Category: Neutral
Reason: The feedback pairs praise for the application's ease of use with a complaint about its slow loading speed.

COMPARISON:
Both outputs classified it as Neutral. Both were correct for this input. Few-shot did not change the classification in this one test, but supplied examples and a response pattern.

LIMITATION:
Only one feedback example was tested. Experiments 1 and 2 were run in the same Gemini conversation, so earlier context may have influenced later responses.
